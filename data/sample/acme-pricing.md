# Acme Cloud pricing

This is fictional sample data for demonstrating the voice agent.

## Starter plan

The Starter plan costs ₹499 per month. It includes 1 user and up to 100 invoices per month. Support is by email.

## Growth plan

The Growth plan costs ₹1,499 per month. It includes up to 5 users and 1,000 invoices per month. It adds automatic payment reminders and recurring invoices. Support is by email and chat.

## Business plan

The Business plan costs ₹3,999 per month. It includes up to 20 users and unlimited invoices. It adds multiple branches, approval workflows and phone support.

## Annual billing

Paying yearly gives a 20 percent discount on every plan compared with monthly billing. Prices do not include 18 percent GST.

## Free trial

Every plan has a 14 day free trial. No credit card is needed to start the trial.

## Cancellation and refunds

Monthly subscriptions can be cancelled at any time and stop at the end of the billing month. Annual subscriptions can be refunded in full within 15 days of purchase. After 15 days there is no refund.
