# Acme Cloud product guide

This is fictional sample data for demonstrating the voice agent.

## What it does

Acme Cloud is online billing and invoicing software for small businesses. Users create GST invoices, send them by email or WhatsApp, track payments and see reports.

## GST support

Invoices calculate CGST, SGST and IGST automatically from the customer's state. GSTR-1 data can be exported as an Excel or JSON file. The software does not file GST returns directly with the government portal.

## Payments

Customers can pay an invoice through UPI, debit card, credit card or net banking using a payment link. Payment status updates automatically.

## Integrations

Data can be exported to Tally in XML format. A REST API is available only on the Business plan. There is no direct integration with SAP.

## Mobile app

Mobile apps are available for Android and iOS. Invoices can be created offline on Android and are synced when the internet returns. Offline mode is not available on iOS.

## Data security

Data is stored in data centres located in India. It is encrypted in transit and at rest. Daily backups are kept for 30 days.

## Setup and migration

Setup takes about one day. Customer and item lists can be imported from an Excel file. The support team helps with data migration free of charge on the Growth and Business plans.

## Support hours

Support is available Monday to Saturday from 9 am to 7 pm India time. There is no support on Sundays or national holidays.
